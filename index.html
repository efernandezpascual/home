Eduardo Fernández Pascual

Biodiversity | Seed regeneration | Habitat restoration

I am an Associate Professor of Botany at the University of Oviedo and a researcher at the Biodiversity Research Institute (IMIB). I lead research on seed regeneration as a key process for biodiversity and habitat restoration, combining vegetation ecology with open-science approaches and biocultural perspectives.

Research

  • SeedAnthropic: Seed regeneration in anthropogenic and invaded habitats.
  • SeedArc: A global archive of primary seed germination data.
  • My publications in ORCID, Google Scholar, and ResearchGate.
  • My datasets & code are available in GitHub. Also check the SylvanSeeds database and the seedr R package.
